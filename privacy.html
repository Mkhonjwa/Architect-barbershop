Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At The Architect Mobile Barbershop, we respect the privacy of our customers and are committed to protecting their personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how we handle it when you visit our website or book our barbering services.

  1. Information We Collect

    When you use our website, contact our team or book an appointment, we may collect information that you provide to us, including:

    • Your full name.
    • Your phone number and email address, if provided.
    • Your preferred haircut or grooming service.
    • Your preferred appointment date and time.
    • Your service location or address, if provided.
    • Any additional information you include in your enquiry.

    We may also receive basic technical information when you visit our website, such as your browser type and the pages you access, depending on the website tools and services being used.

  2. How We Use Your Information

    The information collected helps us provide reliable mobile barbering services and communicate effectively with our customers.

    We may use your information to:

    • Receive, organize and manage haircut bookings.
    • Contact customers about appointment requests and booking arrangements.
    • Confirm appointment dates, times and service details.
    • Identify the location where the customer would like the barbering service provided.
    • Respond to questions, complaints and customer enquiries.
    • Inform customers about changes to appointments or service availability.
    • Improve the quality and efficiency of our barbering services.
    • Maintain the security and proper operation of our website.
    • Meet applicable legal requirements where necessary.

  3. Location Information

    Because The Architect Mobile Barbershop provides mobile services, customers may be asked to provide their service location when making a booking.

    If you choose to use the location-sharing feature on our website, your browser may request permission to access your device's location. You can choose whether to allow or deny this request.

    Location information is used to help our team understand where the requested service should take place. You may also provide your location manually through an appropriate link or website.

  4. How We Protect Your Information

    We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information provided by our customers. We aim to prevent unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure of customer information.

    Access to booking information should be limited to people who need it to arrange and provide our services.

    However, no website or method of electronic communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information through online forms or unsecured communication channels.

  5. Sharing of Personal Information

    We do not intend to sell customers' personal information to other businesses.

    Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate our website, manage bookings or communicate with customers. We may also disclose information where required by law.

  6. Cookies and Website Information

    Our website may use cookies or similar technologies if these are enabled by the website or services connected to it. Cookies are small files stored on your device that can help a website remember certain settings and improve its functionality.

    Depending on the technologies used, cookies may help us understand general website activity, remember preferences and improve the browsing experience.

    You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

  7. Third-Party Websites and Services

    Our website may contain links to external websites or services, such as mapping services used to share a customer's location.

    These third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for how external websites collect or use information. Customers should review those policies before sharing personal information with them.

  8. How Long We Keep Information

    We keep customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage bookings, provide our services, respond to enquiries and meet applicable legal requirements.

    When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or dispose of it securely, where appropriate.

  9. Your Privacy Rights

    Depending on applicable data protection laws, customers may have the right to request access to their personal information, ask for incorrect information to be corrected, or request that information be deleted where legally appropriate.

    Customers may contact our team to make a privacy enquiry or request concerning their information.

  10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices, website features or legal requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
